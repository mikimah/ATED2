Dr. Vijay Kumar Burugari

Professor, Department of Computer Science and Engineering,

KLEF, KL University, India

Biography

Dr. Vijay Kumar Burugari is a Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering KLEF, KL University, India. He holds a Ph.D in Information and Communication Engineering from Anna University.

Professional Contributions

He has authored 35+ Publications and Books chapters in reputed international journals and conferences. He has 3 patents. He is the Co-Founder of Revannor Private Limited. He serves as the coordinator for the placement cell and supported Training and Placement. He has organized several events like seminars, workshops, and Hackathons.

Research

His current research includes Precision Agriculture, wireless sensor network, drone-based disaster management, and Precision Planting using drone. He is guiding 3 PhD scholars in smart-agriculture and image-processing. He is also delivering training & research in: wireless sensor network-security, AI and IoT, Precision Agriculture, Machine Learning & Deep Learning.

Awards

He is a certified in “Data Scientist Mastery Award for Educators 2017 V2” with IBM, Excellence Teaching in Higher Education Award in 2019 by DK International Research Foundation.
