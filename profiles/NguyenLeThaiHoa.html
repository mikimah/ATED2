Dr. Nguyen Le Thai Hoa

Marketing Lecturer in Faculty of Economics and Management,

Saigon Technology University (STU), Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam.

Biography

Nguyen Le Thai Hoa is a Marketing Lecturer in Faculty of Economics and Management, Saigon Technology University (STU), Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. His expertise includes Retailing, Marketing, International Business and Branding.

Professional Contributions

He is CEO of Hoa Mai Trading Company., LTD with Ohiama brand (Eco-friendly products, home appliances and wood tablewares). He also serves as Marketing Director of QMMC Entertainment Company.
