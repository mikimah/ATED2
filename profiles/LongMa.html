Assoc. Prof. Dr. Long Ma

Department of Computer Science,

Troy University.

Biography

Dr. Long Ma is an Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science at Troy University, where he has been a faculty member since 2017. He received his Ph.D. in Computer Science from Georgia State University in 2017.

Research

Dr. Ma’s research interests focus on Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Science, and Big Data Analytics, with particular emphasis on data mining and machine learning. His work combines algorithm development with practical applications, developing data-driven solutions to problems across diverse disciplines. He has collaborated with researchers across academia and with industry professionals, with research applications spanning intelligence analysis, social network analysis, software engineering, and biomedical science.

Professional Contributions

Prior to joining Troy University, Dr. Ma worked with the Applied Machine Learning team at CareerBuilder as a Data Scientist intern from 2016 to 2017. He has published his research in peer-reviewed journals and international conference proceedings in the fields of data science, artificial intelligence, and machine learning.
