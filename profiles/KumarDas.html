Dr. Pradip Kumar Das

Professor - Department of Computer Science & Engineering,

IIT Guwahati, Assam, India

Biography

Dr. Pradip Kumar Das is from IIT Guwahati and is currently the Coordinator of Drone Technology in the School of Interdisciplinary Studies and Sustainability and a professor in the Department of Computer Science & Engineering in IIT Guwahati. He has held the position of Head - Centre for Drone Technology, Organizing Vice Chairman IIT JEE 2009, Vice President IIT Club. He has over 28 years of teaching and research experience in areas like Speech and Image Processing, smart devices and Software Engineering.

Professional Contributions

He is a CSIR NET qualified JRF/SRF fellow and worked in CEERI, Delhi for 5 years and as scientist fellow in HRD group of CSIR (Automation section) for about 2 years. He is a DGCA certified Drone Pilot and instructor for the small and medium categories of drones. He has published more than 100 papers in international journals and conferences in India and abroad. He has executed 16 sponsored projects and consultancies from agencies like MHRD, Dept of Electronics, DST, ICMR, Ministry of Social Justice, UNICEF, etc.

Research

His research interests include speech recognition, analysis and characterization, image processing, Internet of things, AI, smart devices, Drone Technology, algorithms and software engineering.
