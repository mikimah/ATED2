Dr. Prabha Selvaraj

Professor in the School of Computer Science & Engineering,

VIT-AP University (India)

Biography

Dr. Prabha Selvaraj is a Professor in the School of Computer Science & Engineering at VIT-AP University, India.

She holds a Ph.D. in Information and Communication Engineering from Anna University. She is the Founder & CSO of Revannor Private Limited..

Professional Contributions

She serves as the coordinator for the Centre of Excellence in Blockchain at VIT-AP. She has organized several events like seminar, workshop, industry summit and Hackathons in collaboration with industry. She previously served as Head of the Department in MRIET. She trained students in skill development. She has been involved in curriculum development for a few courses and Quality Control Management.

Research
  • Medical Image Processing & Computer Vision
  • AI, IoT& Security, Unmanned Aerial Systems
  • Recommendation System and Optimization

She has authored 45+ works in reputed international journals and conferences. She has 11 patents. Her current research includes medical image processing, drone-based disaster management, stealth based autonomous vehicle, emergency response system and Precision Planting using drones. She is guiding 4 PhD scholars in medical image processing, disease prediction and smart agriculture
