Faran Lodhi

Partner Architect,

CG Global, FLAAA UK.

Biography

Faran Lodhi is an internationally experienced architect, author and strategic design thinker with 35 years of architectural practice across the UK, Saudi Arabia, Pakistan and Southeast Asia. His work explores the convergence of climate-responsive architecture, cultural context, sustainability, technology and AI-native design.

Research

He approaches architecture as a strategic and diagnostic discipline, understanding climate, culture, site, human needs and technical constraints before design begins. He combines this approach with AI-assisted design and emerging digital workflows to develop intelligent, resilient, resource-conscious and minimalist architectural solutions across residential, hospitality, commercial and mixed-use projects.

Professional Contributions

Faran Lodhi is the author of AI & Architecture: Designing the Future of the Built World and has extensive experience in architectural education, practice and international design collaboration.
