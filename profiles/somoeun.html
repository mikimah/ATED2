Asst. Prof. Somoeun Mut

Head of Global Engagement and Partnerships,

University of Technology and Entrepreneurship (UTE),

Phnom Penh, Cambodia.

Biography

Somoeun Mut is an experienced Cambodian academic leader, educational policy specialist, and researcher in higher education administration, global partnerships, and educational technology. He currently serves as the Head of Global Engagement and Partnerships at the University of Technology and Entrepreneurship (UTE) in Phnom Penh, where he directs international strategic alliances, institutional branding, and stakeholder engagement.

Research

His research spans Buddhist economics, humane AI ethics, Khmer culture, school-based management, and higher education curriculum development, with recent scholarly work exploring Eastern moral perspectives on conflict resolution, sustainability, and emerging technologies.

Professional Contributions

His extensive administrative career includes senior leadership roles across Cambodian higher education, notably as Vice Dean of the Faculty of Arts, Humanities and Social Sciences and Head of the Academic and International Affairs Office at CamTech University, as well as Associate Dean of the College of Education at The University of Cambodia. He also served as an Official at the Department of Policy within the Ministry of Education, Youth and Sport (MoEYS), contributing to key national initiatives such as the Teacher Career Pathway Framework, the National School Feeding Policy, the Equity-Focused Primary Scholarship Framework, and School-Based Management.
