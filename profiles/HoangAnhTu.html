Dr. Architect

Hoang Anh Tu

University of Architecture,

Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam.

Biography

Dr. Architect Hoang Anh Tu is an architect, urbanist, researcher, and lecturer at the University of Architecture Ho Chi Minh City (UAH), Vietnam. His interdisciplinary academic background spans architecture, landscape architecture, urban design, urban planning, and urban studies, with education and research experience in Vietnam, France, and Canada. He holds a Ph.D. in Urban Planning and completed postdoctoral research in Urban Studies at the Institut National de la Recherche Scientifique (INRS), Canada.

Professional Contributions

With over three decades of academic and professional experience, Dr. Hoang has taught and supervised students at undergraduate, master’s, and doctoral levels in Vietnam and Canada. His research interests include urban studies, sustainable urban development, urban resilience, environmental and spatial equity, green infrastructure, landscape planning, public space, and urban heritage. Alongside his academic career, Dr. Hoang has extensive professional experience in architecture, urban design, and planning, contributing to projects ranging from urban and ecological planning to institutional, hospitality, and public architecture. Bridging academic research and professional practice, his work addresses contemporary urban challenges with a particular focus on creating cities that are more sustainable, resilient, inclusive, and human-centered.
